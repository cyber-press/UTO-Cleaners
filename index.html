Our Meticulous Process

Transparency and quality are at the heart of what we do. Follow your garment's journey from your closet back to you, fresh and clean.

Step 1

Drop-off or Pickup

Your journey begins when you either drop off your items at one of our convenient branches or schedule a pickup with our friendly drivers. Upon receipt, we conduct an initial inspection for any tears, stains, or special requests you've noted, ensuring we start with a clear understanding of your needs. An invoice is printed in triplicate for perfect record-keeping.

Step 2

Tagging & Identification

To prevent any mix-ups, every item is meticulously tagged. We assign your unique customer ID, note the branch of origin, and log a detailed description of each garment, including its color and designer. This crucial step ensures your items are tracked accurately throughout our entire process.

Step 3

Dispatch to Factory

Once logged, your items are carefully placed in dispatch bags and transported to our central cleaning factory. Our driver logs every item in a dispatch book, and upon arrival, factory staff confirm the counts and perform a second inspection. Any discrepancies or newly found issues are photographed and communicated to you immediately via WhatsApp for full transparency.

Your Questions, Answered

What if a stain doesn't come out?+

While we are stain removal experts, some stains can be permanent. If we encounter a particularly stubborn stain, we will notify you immediately to discuss potential solutions or next steps. We will never proceed with a treatment that could damage your garment without your consent.

How is billing handled for pickups?+

For pickup orders, we will send you a detailed digital invoice via WhatsApp or email after your items have been inspected and logged at our facility. You can pay securely online through a payment link. Your items will be scheduled for delivery once payment is confirmed.

What is the 'Order Map'?+

The Order Map is our internal quality control document that follows your order from start to finish. At each stage—sorting, cleaning, ironing, packaging—our staff sign off with their name and the date. This ensures accountability and a flawless finish for every item.
